Verdict
01

Quarantine

A Gmail filter diverts all incoming mail out of the inbox into a quarantine label. Only the gateway moves mail back.

02

Triage

Mail with no links and no red flags goes straight through. Anything with links goes on to be tested.

03

Detonation

Links open in a throwaway Daytona sandbox. Forms are filled with a decoy identity backed by a fake customer in the Nessie bank API.

04

Verdict

If the page harvests card, bank or login details, the mail is blocked and we record where the scammer sends the data.

Three possible verdicts

Each message ends up in exactly one place.

Released

Safe

No links and no red flags, or the sandbox found the links harmless. Delivered to your inbox.

Blocked

Scam

The page asked for card, CVV, SSN or bank details, or posted data to an exfiltration endpoint. Moved away and never shown.

Held

Unverified

The page was down or the sandbox failed. It stays out of your inbox until you review it.

Safety model

The sandbox only ever sees fake data.

Disposable

Each link opens in an ephemeral sandbox that is auto-deleted and never reused. Downloads are cancelled, dialogs dismissed and popups closed.

Decoy data only

Luhn-valid card numbers from a reserved test range and 9xx SSNs. None of your data ever touches a scam page.

Locked down

Containers run non-root and read-only with all capabilities dropped. Secrets stay in server-side env files.
